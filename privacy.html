1. Who we are

Manghat Dairy Farm sells fresh milk and delivers it to homes in and around Ghulam Muhammad Abad, Faisalabad. The farm is run by Dr. Qurban Ali Manghat. This policy explains what the Manghat Dairy app collects and how it is used.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number and home address. Your mobile number is also your login, and your password is stored securely by Firebase Authentication. We never see it.
  • Orders and bills: your orders, subscription, deliveries, quantities, amounts, cash collected by the delivery man and payment messages you send us.
  • Reviews: the ratings and comments you write. Other customers see your first name, your rating and your comment.
  • Home location (optional): saved only if you tap “Save my home location”. It is used to show distance and arrival time on the delivery map.
  • Delivery staff location: for our delivery men only. Their phone shares its location while a delivery route is active, so that customers waiting for a delivery and the dairy owner can see it. Sharing stops when the route is finished.

3. Why we use it

  • To take and deliver your orders, and to keep your bill correct.
  • To show delivery progress on a map.
  • To contact you about your delivery.
  • To show customer reviews and let the owner reply.

We do not sell your data, we do not show advertisements, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Where it is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it for us. Access inside the app is limited by role: you can see your own information, delivery staff see the deliveries assigned to them, and the dairy owner can see customer and order records in order to run the business.

When you view a map, map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which may receive your device’s IP address. Delivery staff may open an address in Google Maps for directions.

5. Deleting your data

In the app, open Account, then Delete my account, and enter your password. This deletes your login, profile, subscription and reviews.

Past delivery and payment records are kept for the dairy’s accounts, because they show what was delivered and paid. They are not used for anything else.

If you cannot use the app, call or send a WhatsApp message to 03289613699 and ask us to delete your account.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is controlled by security rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults who order milk for their household. It is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
